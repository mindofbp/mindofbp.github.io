Welcome!

I am an Applied and Computational Math MS student at the University of Washington. Previously, I earned my BS in Mathematics from Florida State University.

I am currently advised by Prof. Heather Wilber, and am working on singular quadrature in the context of boundary integral equations. Broadly speaking, my interests are in the combination of scientific computing and mathematics, particularly for building fast ways to conduct physical simulations.

Before returning to school, I was a software engineer building tools to improve audio editing.

Outside of academics, I try to take full advantage of living in the PNW. In my few moments of free time, I run, bike, hike, and ski as much as I can.
